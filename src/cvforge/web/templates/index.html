Welcome to cvforge 👋

Three quick steps: connect an AI model you already have, paste your career data, pick your defaults. Then switch to Create CV and paste a job offer.

1 · AI model

cvforge doesn't include an AI — it uses one you already have. Tools found on this computer are marked ● found.

2 · Your career data (source of truth)

Write everything about your career once — jobs, achievements with numbers, skills, education, languages. Any language is fine. cvforge picks what fits each job and never invents facts. Private notes go in <!-- comments -->; names that must never appear go in the ## cvforge rules section.
